# EXP002 research plan — specified before implementation

Date: 2026-10-13. Base: EXP001 commit 9564602, draft PR #1. Read before implementation: EXP001 model.py, analysis.py, AUDIT.md, METHODOLOGY.md and RESULTS.md. This is an investigation of competing modeled explanations, not an assumption that harm is negative environmental transfer. EXP001 and V1 files remain unchanged.

## Audit findings relevant to identification

Default knowledge_filter_mode=filter rejects cross-zone direct sharing AND neighbor reads. With all memory initialized empty, foreign-zone observations cannot enter default worlds. Thus environmental cross-zone mismatch is not a necessary cause of EXP001's observed harm. Same-zone farms still differ in goals, land, cost noise and suitability noise; the model does not give them different within-zone soil/climate.

Harvest tuples lack source IDs and dates. Historical tuple identities cannot safely be inferred from equal numeric values. EXP002 will assign immutable provenance prospectively at the actual harvest event (seed, farmer, season), maintaining V1 numerical tuple order and multiplicity in the unchanged treatment. Propagation sends only the latest own harvest, not recursively forwarding entire histories; however neighbor reads merge histories containing earlier direct receipts, and the same event can appear via multiple neighbors and the own-experience channel. These are different forms of double counting, not an indefinitely recursive broadcast algorithm.

The zone score mixes normalized gross yield (60%) with nonnegative normalized total profit (40%), adds .45 times that mixture, and penalizes unobserved crops. Neighbor and experience objectives weight yield, low costs and low water according to four fixed goal profiles; they do not maximize net profit. Own experience is normalized across observed crops. Without exploration an initially isolated farmer observes one crop and its normalized bonus remains zero. Water lookup still falls back to 500 mm; no correction is allowed here.

D2 also separates zone-only, neighbor-only and combined information using EXP001's existing shared_strategy definitions. Neighbor-only still includes received tuples in experience; zone-only has no neighbor propagation. These semantics must be labeled, not silently redesigned.

## Fixed design

Reproduce all four EXP001 primary conditions A–D, seeds 0–19, 100 farmers/condition, 30 seasons, original parameters. Diagnostic treatments use economics OFF and change one item relative to B, except D1 changes exploration relative to A. Sixteen arms: A, B, C, D; A_e02, A_e05, A_e10, A_e20; zone_only; neighbor_only; dedup; own_memory_only; profit_per_ha; unrestricted; ecological; zone_profit_only. Smoke first: six farmers, three seasons, seeds 0 and 1; then the full design if feasible.

Reuse EXP001 semantic RNG streams for all population/weather/harvest/order/suitability draws. Exploration gets separate keys (explore, season, farmer) and (explore_choice, season, farmer), shared across rates; it cannot advance exogenous streams. Ecological filtering uses static pre-outcome environmental inputs. No future harvest is supplied to decisions. Post-decision counterfactual profit uses the same already-defined harvest shock and is an analyst-only diagnostic.

Instrumentation must reproduce EXP001 A–D exactly with intervention defaults and with tracing on/off. Score decomposition records the change applied at each step in the original operation order (including negative zone penalties). Component sums may differ from the original accumulated score by floating-point rounding only; argmax uses original operation order. Immutable provenance is generated from reruns, never retroactively fabricated for existing CSVs.

## Estimands, multiplicity and reporting

Seed is the independent unit. Average seasons per identity, then equal-weight farmers per seed. Report mean/median profits, mean and area-weighted yields, harm/benefit fractions, unconditional negative-part loss, conditional harmed severity (missing when no harmed farmers), lower-tail effect quantiles, switches per eligible season, information/provenance counts and per-zone estimates. Farmer-season and transition summaries are descriptive; no independent-observation CIs.

Primary diagnostic contrasts: each of the eight B-based interventions versus B and each of four exploration arms versus A. For each of 12 contrasts estimate two endpoints (mean profit and fraction harmed relative to original paired A), overall and ARID: one prespecified family of 48 two-sided tests. Use Holm correction across that entire family. Report unadjusted 95% seed t intervals and Holm-adjusted p-values distinctly; do not call unadjusted intervals simultaneous intervals. All other zones, endpoints, transition decompositions, correlations and subgroup comparisons are exploratory. Conditional harmed groups are post-treatment selections, so associations are not causal mechanisms. If smoke has too few seeds, uncertainty is diagnostic only.

For each B-based intervention report both delta versus B and outcomes/harm relative to A. For D1 also report B minus exploratory baseline to distinguish changed comparison baseline from improved information treatment. Severity and rescues among originally harmed farmers are descriptive; regression/selection effects must not be mistaken for an independent causal population claim.

## Completion and integrity

Preserve raw outcomes, compressed decision/candidate traces, provenance ledger, seed summaries, configs, environment and source/input hashes. Commit compact evidence, not large raw traces. Tests cover equivalence, timing, provenance/deduplication, intervention independence, economics normalization, context rules, RNG pairing, aggregation and EXP001 reproduction. Report positive, zero, negative and contradictory results; uncertainties may prevent a unique explanation. Keep the water fallback, avoid RL/trust, and end with a prioritized EXP003 proposal without implementing it. Open a dependent draft PR targeting research/exp001-social-learning-ablation, never main.
